# One owner, who gives outcomes and makes business calls

handh.dev has one owner. The owner's input is exactly three things: what is wanted, in plain words; a reaction to the visible result; and business calls. Everything else belongs to agents. People at the companies ask the owner, and the owner tells the agents.

Business calls are: spending money, what a company is promised, names and brands, and anything involving a real person outside handh.dev.

Any new cost is a business call every time. A new cost is anything that adds a line to the bill: a bigger server, a paid service, a domain, a subscription. An existing bill drifting because existing things were used more is not a new cost.

Agents send the owner one plain-language daily note, and message immediately in two cases: a business call is waiting, or something is broken and they could not fix it.

## Considered options

- **A monthly spending ceiling agents can use freely.** Rejected by the owner in favour of deciding each new cost.
- **An owner per company.** Not now. Work is tagged by company so this can be switched on later.

## Consequences

- Agents work inside the resources that already exist and stop to ask when they cannot. The single server's memory is the first limit they will meet.
