# handh.dev

handh.dev is the umbrella that builds and runs the technical side of several companies. This is the shared language for how it ships and maintains what it runs.

## Language

**Magical**:
The owner says what a company needs in plain words and it is live: no code, no approvals, no waiting, and anything can be undone. Work is judged by whether it shortens the distance between the owner saying something and it being real.
_Avoid_: Polished, delightful, seamless

### Roles

**Company**:
A business whose technical side handh.dev builds and runs, such as Bubba's Fireworks or Fat Man Wholesale.
_Avoid_: Client, customer, tenant

**Owner**:
The one person who says what is wanted, reacts to the visible result, and makes business calls. The owner does not review or approve how anything is built.
_Avoid_: Admin, reviewer, approver, operator

**Agent**:
An AI that builds, ships, fixes and maintains everything handh.dev runs, on its own authority and without waiting on a person.
_Avoid_: Assistant, bot, copilot

**Business call**:
A decision only the owner makes: spending money, what a company is promised, names and brands, and anything involving a real person outside handh.dev.
_Avoid_: Approval, sign-off, review

**Daily note**:
A short plain-language summary agents send the owner each day: what shipped, what broke and was fixed, and what is waiting on a business call.
_Avoid_: Report, receipt, changelog, status update

### Safety

**Undo**:
The ability to return anything an agent changed to how it was before. It is the safety mechanism that replaces approval.
_Avoid_: Rollback approval, review gate

**Protected backup**:
A copy of data that nobody, agent or owner, can delete or alter for a set period. It is the one thing agents cannot touch.
_Avoid_: Snapshot, archive
